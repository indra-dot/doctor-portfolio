              I am an Internal Medicine Resident at RSUP Prof. Dr. I.G.N.G. Ngoerah / Udayana University. My daily clinical practice centers on inpatient care, acute admissions, and subspecialty wards, with focused research interests in hematology and gastroenterology.
            

            

              When dealing with critical calculations—such as nephrotoxic drug dosing in acute kidney injury, complex electrolyte deficits, or multi-criteria prognostic scores—mental math and cumbersome PDF guidelines introduce unnecessary friction. I engineer standalone, privacy-first software tools to deliver instantaneous, guideline-adherent decisions at the point of care.
            

            

              "The most impactful digital health innovations emerge where clinical precision meets efficient, dependable code."

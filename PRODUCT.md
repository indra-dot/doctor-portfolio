## Flagship Products & Tooling
1. **InternaHub CDSS:** Single-file offline-capable clinical decision support suite for Google Sites embed, featuring 40+ validated scores across Nephrology, Cardiology, Hepatology, Rheumatology, and Critical Care.
2. **IPD CBT Pro:** Computer-based examination simulator and 1,000+ question bank for Internal Medicine specialist board preparation.
3. **Pindai Sehat:** AI vision nutrient scanner and medication safety checker.
4. **Internize.ai:** On-device medical language processing, HIPAA Safe Harbor de-identification, and FHIR R4 Bundle generation.
5. **Dompetku:** Privacy-first personal cashflow and budget tracker for resident doctors.

## Key Design & Safety Requirements
- **100% Privacy by Design:** Zero patient identifiable information (PHI) leaves the client device.
- **Evidence-Based Medical Rigor:** Formulas bench-tested against gold-standard literature (KDIGO 2024, Sanford Guide 2024, Stanford Antimicrobial Dosing, NEJM Adrogué-Madias, ACR/EULAR).

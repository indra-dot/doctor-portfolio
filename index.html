About Me

Clinician, clinical researcher and healthcare software developer, working where evidence-based medicine meets software engineering.

dr. Ida Bagus Indra Wibawa Putrawan, S.Ked
Internal Medicine Resident
Denpasar, Bali, Indonesia

Clinician who codes: building practical clinical decision support systems.

I am an Internal Medicine Resident at RSUP Prof. Dr. I.G.N.G. Ngoerah / Udayana University in Denpasar, Bali. My clinical practice and research focus on internal medicine, with a special interest in hematology and gastroenterology.

The friction I meet at the bedside, on inpatient wards and in emergency rooms is what drives me to build clinical decision support systems (CDSS). Each one is designed to help clinicians make evidence-based decisions quickly, accurately and safely.

"I believe the most impactful healthcare innovations emerge where clinical precision meets efficient code."
